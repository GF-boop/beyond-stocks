\caption{Capital preservation and income funding. The figure plots the probability of failing to fund the
path-matched income against the probability of financial ruin for the all-equity, proportional, and risk
parity strategies at gross exposures from 100\% to 200\%. The probability of financial ruin applies the 4\%
rule to each strategy's own retirement wealth. The path-matched income is 4\% of the retirement wealth of the
all-equity strategy at 100\% exposure on the same simulated path. Strategies above the dashed line fund the
path-matched income less reliably than they preserve their own capital. Results are based on 10,000
bootstrap simulations from the 1927 to 2025 sample with a 10\% savings rate.}
\label{fig:income-ruin}
