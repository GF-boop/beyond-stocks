Panel E scales each diversified strategy to the 25.28\% volatility of the unlevered all-equity strategy. In Panels D and E, the first column names the strategy, and gross exposure is 100\% plus borrowing. Stocks are 33\% domestic and 67\% international. Borrowing is financed at the local bill rate plus 0.30\%. Volatility is the standard deviation of annual real portfolio returns in the pooled panel. Each comparison is based on 10,000 bootstrap simulations of household outcomes that share return, earnings, and mortality draws. The household saves 10\% of labor income. The probability of financial ruin is the probability of exhausting wealth before the death of the last survivor under the 4\% rule. The equivalent savings rate is the savings rate that equates expected utility from retirement consumption and bequest with that of the all-equity strategy at 100\% exposure and a 10\% savings rate.\end{minipage}
