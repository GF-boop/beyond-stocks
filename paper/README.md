# Manuscript sources

The current manuscript is the ERC refocusing:
[`new_paper/main-styled.pdf`](new_paper/main-styled.pdf). Its source
(`new_paper/main-styled.tex`, `utility-framework.tex`,
`appendices-refocused.tex`, `references.bib`, `references-erc.bib`) and its
figures live under [`new_paper/`](new_paper/). The build and replication guide
is [`new_paper/README.md`](new_paper/README.md), and the frozen design of the
main experiment is
[`../results/erc_refocusing/PROTOCOL.md`](../results/erc_refocusing/PROTOCOL.md).

[`figures/`](figures/) holds shared generated LaTeX tables included by the
appendices; they are produced by the `render_*` and `build_*_data.py` scripts
described in [`../build/README.md`](../build/README.md) and
[`new_paper/README.md`](new_paper/README.md).

Earlier manuscript generations (`paper/archive/`, `paper/main-styled.tex`,
`paper/beyond_stocks/`, `paper/theory_first/`) are abandoned and no longer part
of the repository.
